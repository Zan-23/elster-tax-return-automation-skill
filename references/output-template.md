# Output template

Use this structure for the final answer, after intake and verification. If a document artifact is available, put it there and keep it updated; keep the chat reply to the headline and the open items.

## 1. Headline
- Tax year, filing type (mandatory or voluntary) and the deadline that applies (with source)
- **Estimated refund or payment**, plus a range if scenarios remain
- The one or two items that would change it most

## 2. Breakdown table

| # | Item | Form → section | Calculation / source | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Group the rows and show subtotals:
- gross income (by source)
- each work expense, and the total compared with the flat allowance
- insurance and pension contributions (prefilled values)
- special expenses, and the total compared with the flat allowance
- taxable income
- assessed tax, and taxes withheld
- **refund or payment**

Add a scenario table for uncertain items, and the marginal effect per 100 € of extra deductions.

## 3. ELSTER field mapping

| Form | Section in ELSTER | Line / field label (selected year) | Value | Justification | Prefilled? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- Take line numbers and labels from the selected year's form or the live ELSTER page.
- Mark certificate values as "prefilled: verify, don't re-enter".

## 4. Not entered (and why)

Each excluded item with a reason, for example:
- private cost
- reimbursed or employer-paid
- below the user's own share
- covered by another allowance
- already taxed and no correction wanted

## 5. Assumptions and evidence to keep
- Day counts, business-use shares and placeholders, so the user can verify them
- Receipts and logs to keep, and for how long (check the current retention guidance)

## 6. Glossary
German term → plain English, for the terms used above.

## 7. Sources
The official sources for each year-sensitive value, rule and field, with the date checked.

One line: this is an estimate, not tax advice; ELSTER's preliminary calculation and the Bescheid are authoritative.
